# 改写配方（before → after）

每条配方：适用场景 / 模板 / 仿写示例（我写的，非 Dourish 原文）/ 已核验原文例证（附出处，仅供参照，**不得抄入稿件**）。
所有 before 例句为典型"平庸学术英语"构造。

## P1 否定—重述（优先级最高）
适用：thesis 句、定义、贡献声明、intro 段尾。
模板：`X is not simply/just [主流理解], but [实践性/关系性重述].` / `..., but rather ...` / `... rather than ...`
仿写：
- before: "Context is an important factor for system design."
- after: "Context here is not simply a feature of the environment to be captured, but an achievement of the interaction itself."

原文例证："affect is not a representational state to be transferred from one place to another, but rather is an aspect of collectively enacted social settings"（Affect 2005）

## P2 属性→成就（属性谓词改成就谓词）
适用：把名词化静态判断改成过程判断（Register A）。
模板：`X is not a property of [objects]; it is a property/achievement of [practice/interaction].`
仿写：
- before: "Reproducibility is a characteristic of well-documented pipelines."
- after: "Reproducibility is not a property of pipelines but an achievement of the communities who rerun them."

原文例证："Embodiment is not a property of systems, technologies, or artifacts; it is a property of interaction."（WtAI 2004）

## P3 the ways in which 框架
适用：研究问题句、intro 的 gap 陈述。
模板：`... the ways in which [actors] [verb] [object] in/through [setting].`
仿写：
- before: "We study how developers use CI tools."
- after: "We examine the ways in which developers encounter, domesticate, and routinize CI tooling in the course of everyday work."

原文例证："the ways in which representational forms, their associated material properties, and knowledge practices are coupled"（2017, ch.1）

## P4 教学式改写对
适用：引入术语后、模型/框架定义后。
模板：`[术语句]. That is, [具体、日常语言重述].`
仿写：
- before: "Our approach operationalizes configurational validity."
- after: "Our approach operationalizes configurational validity. That is, we ask not whether a measure is correct in the abstract, but what work it does in the settings where it is deployed."

原文例证："the challenges that probes pose are epistemological ones. That is, cultural probes are not simply 'another technique' for getting data..."（Probes 2007）

## P5 复数量词替换单数
适用：讨论原因/因素/维度的一切句子。
- "the reason" → "a range of reasons" / "a set of factors"
- "types of users" → "different sorts of users"
仿写：
- before: "The main cause of tool abandonment is poor onboarding."
- after: "Tool abandonment arises from a series of frictions — onboarding, maintenance, and the drift between the tool and local practice."

## P6 抽象→具象锚点
适用：段落里连续 ≥2 句无实例时。
模板：抽象论点后接一个带物理细节的画面（重量、体积、身体动作、空间位置）。
仿写：
- before: "Storing large datasets was expensive for early institutions."
- after: "Storing large datasets was expensive in the most literal sense: one gigabyte on punch cards weighed more than thirty-five tons."

原文例证："One gigabyte of data on punch cards weighs more than thirty-five tons."（2017, ch.1）

## P7 成对设问开场（理论文/intro）
适用：intro 第一段、discussion 转折处。只设真问题，不打稻草人。
模板：`What does it mean to [动词] [对象]? What comes into view, and what drops out of view, when we [做法]?`
仿写：
- before: "In this section, we discuss LLM agents."
- after: "What does it mean to call an agent 'autonomous'? What is in view, and out of view, when we focus on autonomy rather than, say, accountability?"

原文例证："What does it mean to adopt the algorithm as an object of analytic attention? What is in view, and out of view, when we focus on the algorithm?"（Algorithms 2016）

## P8 轶事开场（仅可用用户稿件里的真实材料）
适用：intro 第一段。要素：具体时间/地点/物件 + 一个小失败或意外 + 由它引出概念问题。**禁止编造轶事；无真实材料时改用 P7。**
仿写：
- before: "Data governance is increasingly important in organizations."
- after: "In March 2024, we watched a city planner paste a URL into a shared spreadsheet and wait, as she put it, 'for the number to become official.' The number never came. This paper is about what happened next."

原文例证："During my time as an undergraduate student in computer science, algorithms were objects of concern in a variety of ways..."（Algorithms 2016, Introduction）

## P9 限定语校准
适用：讨论/limitations/claims。
- 断言事实：不用限定语
- 判断/解释：might/perhaps/arguably + 一个 not-X-but-Y 收紧
仿写：
- before: "This shows that algorithms are biased."
- after: "What this suggests, perhaps, is not that the algorithm is biased in some general sense, but that its biases are legible only against the specific practices of appeal that surround it."

## P10 in the course of（Register A 专用）
- "when users collaborate" → "in the course of collaboration"
- "during use" → "in the course of their use"

## P11 标题改写
- 交错配列：`The X of Y and the Y of X`
- 头韵/对仗：`Divining a Digital Future` 型
- 戏仿（慎用，需 venue 宽容）：`What we talk about when we talk about X`
仿写："Codifying Care and Caring for Code"、"The Datafication of Work and the Work of Datafication"

## P12 数据能动性句式（data 叙事论文）
模板：`Data do not speak for themselves; they must be [动词] — [展开].`
仿写：
- before: "Our dashboard visualizes usage data."
- after: "Usage data do not speak for themselves; the dashboard is best understood as a stage on which they must be narrated."

原文例证："Data do not speak for themselves. Data must be narrated—put to work in particular contexts, sunk into narratives that give them shape and meaning..."（Datafication 2018, Abstract）
