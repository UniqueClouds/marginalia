## Dataset file and scope

[Download the complete compressed JSON (about 29.6 MB)](../data/nsf-awards-priority-2026-11-03.json.gz). It contains 19,630 award records, full English abstracts, Chinese abstract translations where the source corpus already had them, PI/co-PI names, institutions, amounts, NSF programs and organizations, dates, topic labels, award links, and bibliographic metadata listed by NSF records. Thirty-four records have no English abstract; 43 already have a Chinese translation.

The records come from the 2026-11-03 local filtered snapshot and preserve the previously defined subject scope, amount threshold, and exclusions, with the student-specific funding exception retained. “Research areas” are curated labels for this collection, not official NSF classifications. Publication entries are bibliographic outcomes listed by NSF; their presence does not establish that an award was the paper's sole funding source.

NSF says award data and abstract text are in the public domain. This export does not include article full text. [NSF Award Search overview](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/award-search)
